We offer flexible pricing, primarily on an hourly basis, for our Delhi podcast studio rental services. Discounts are available for longer sessions or bulk bookings, making it suitable for both one-time creators and recurring podcasters.

Some studio packages include basic post-production such as audio cleanup or camera angle switching. For advanced services like video editing, branded overlays, titles, and social media optimisation, we offer affordable add-on packages or custom editing solutions.

If you need additional time during your session, we can extend your booking based on studio availability. Extra time is billed in 1-hour increments according to your selected plan.

Yes, we offer special podcast rental packages in Delhi for brands, agencies, production teams, and recurring podcasters. If you need a monthly plan or long-term access, we can create a custom package tailored to your needs.

#### How many people can I bring for a session?
Our premium podcast studio in Delhi can comfortably accommodate 2 to 4 people on camera, depending on the chosen setup. Please inform us in advance if you plan to record with more guests.

#### Are there any hidden charges?
No. We maintain transparent pricing for all our services. Everything included in your selected package is clearly mentioned, and any add-ons or extras are quoted in advance.

#### Can I take a studio tour or test the setup before booking?
Yes. We offer complimentary studio tours of our Delhi podcast studio by prior appointment. You can also book a short trial session at a nominal cost, which can be adjusted in your final package if you proceed with booking.

#### What are your payment options and cancellation terms?
We accept UPI, bank transfers, credit/debit cards, and cash. To confirm your slot, a 50% advance payment is required. The remaining balance is due after your session. For rescheduling or cancellations, please inform us at least 48 hours in advance to avoid cancellation charges.
